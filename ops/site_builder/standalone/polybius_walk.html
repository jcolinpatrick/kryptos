K4 Beaufort Keystream — Polybius Grid Walk

The K4 Beaufort keystream JLJODEGKUKKKLOCGGBGOKTRU shows anomalous clustering when mapped to the 5-wide KRYPTOS-alphabet Polybius grid. Consecutive keystream values land on the same grid row 10 out of 23 times (2.3x the random expectation, p=0.0057).
How to use: Press Play Walk to animate the keystream path across the grid, or use Step to advance one position at a time. The speed slider controls animation pace. Red lines in the walk path indicate same-row transitions. Hover over any keystream cell in the timeline to highlight its position on the grid.
